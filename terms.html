JUMP321 is a fitness tracking tool, not a medical device, and nothing in the app is medical advice. Jump rope is a high-impact activity. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning this or any exercise programme, particularly if you have a heart condition, joint or bone problems, are pregnant, or have any other condition that exercise could affect.

You are solely responsible for exercising safely. Make sure you have enough clear space to swing a rope, a suitable surface, and appropriate footwear, and stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath. Because the app runs on a propped-up phone, take care that your phone is placed where it will not fall or be struck.

Accuracy of Measurements

Jump counts, pace, intensity zones, calorie figures, fat-gram equivalents and all comparison figures shown in the app are estimates produced by computer vision and general formulas. They are approximations intended for motivation and rough tracking. They are not clinically accurate and should not be relied upon for medical, dietary, or competitive purposes. Counting accuracy depends on lighting, camera placement, clothing, and how much of your body is visible, and it may undercount or, less often, miscount.
