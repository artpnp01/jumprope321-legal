Privacy Policy

JUMP321 · Last updated: September 14, 2026

JUMP321 ("the app", "we", "our") is a jump rope counter for iOS. This policy explains what data the app touches, how it's used, and what it never does. We built this app to work entirely on your device — there is no server of ours in the loop.

Camera

The app uses your device's front camera to detect your body position and count jump rope reps, using Apple's on-device Vision framework. Camera frames are processed locally, in real time, and are never saved, recorded, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere — not to us, not to any third party. Nothing from the camera leaves your device.

Photos

If you choose to create a "Selfie Share Card," the app accesses your camera to take that one photo and combines it with your workout stats into an image. That image is only saved to your Photos library if you explicitly tap "Save to Photos," or shared through the system share sheet if you tap "Share." We never access your existing photo library beyond what's needed to save that image.

Workout Data

Your jump counts, session history, durations, and rhythm data are stored locally on your device and synced across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit, tied to your personal iCloud account. This sync is handled entirely by Apple's infrastructure — we do not operate a database or server, and we cannot see, access, or download your workout data. There is no sign-up, login, or account system in this app.

Subscription & Payment

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never receive, see, or store your payment card details. Your purchase and billing history is managed by your Apple ID / App Store account, not by us.

Analytics & Advertising

This app contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries. We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties, because we don't collect any in the first place.

Children's Privacy

JUMP321 is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age — see above: there is no account system and no data leaves your device except through your own iCloud sync.

Deleting Your Data

Since your workout data lives in your device's local storage and your own iCloud account, you're always in control of it:

  • Deleting the app removes its local data from that device.
  • To remove synced data from iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone and remove JUMP321's data.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.
