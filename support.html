The frame never turns green

JUMP321 needs to see both of your shoulders and both of your hips at the same time before it will start counting. The usual fixes:

  • Prop the phone up against something — a wall, a monitor, a stand — with the front camera facing you. Holding it in your hand will not work.
  • Step back about two metres / six feet, until your upper body is well inside the frame.
  • Turn on more light. Low light makes the hip joints hard to detect, and hips are usually what holds the green frame back.
  • Avoid very loose or baggy clothing around the waist.

The count seems low

JUMP321 deliberately stops counting rather than guessing when it cannot see you clearly — it would rather miss a jump than invent one. If you walk out of frame it pauses and keeps the count you already have. If counts are consistently low, try better lighting and a little more distance from the phone.

Calories look wrong

Calorie, fat-gram and comparison figures are estimates based on your body weight and jump count, not clinical measurements. You can change your weight any time by tapping the weight chip at the top of the main screen. Your weight is stored only on your device.

My history is missing on a new device

Sessions sync through your own private iCloud account. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is turned on, then open the app and give it a moment. JUMP321 has no accounts or servers of its own, so there is nothing for us to restore from — the data lives in your iCloud.

Subscriptions and refunds

Your first five sessions are free. After that, JUMP321 Pro unlocks unlimited sessions. Your history, day reports and share cards are never locked, whether or not you subscribe.

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. To view, change or cancel, open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Already paid but the app still asks you to subscribe? Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove synced data from iCloud, go to Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and remove JUMP321's data.
